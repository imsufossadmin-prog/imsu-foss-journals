## ABOUT US

The Faculty of Social Sciences (FOSS) at Imo State University (IMSU), Owerri, Nigeria, is one of the foremost faculties at IMSU. As a distinguished faculty in academic research, FOSS is dedicated to advancing knowledge in the fields of social and behavioural sciences. Over the years, FOSS has built a strong reputation for promoting research, innovation, and academic excellence, with the aim of addressing local, national, and global challenges. FOSS journals are scholarly, refereed, open-access, high-quality and peer-reviewed journals in all social and behavioural sciences fields utilising multi-dimensional research approaches in the conduct of scholarly inquiry. These approaches include theoretical, empirical and experimental methods. 

The FOSS journal platform was developed to house and manage scholarly journals published by the Faculty of Social Sciences. Currently, the platform features three key journals:

- **African Journal of Social and Behavioural Sciences (AJSBS)**: The AJSBS was launched in 2009. Over the years, the AJSBS has become a trusted source of peer-reviewed articles on topics ranging from political science, public administration, public relations, communication, geography, environmental management, information sciences, sociology, psychology, psychotherapy, crime and delinquency, economics, financial management, etc. The AJSBS is well-regarded for its high academic standards and contributions to interdisciplinary research.
- **Global Journal of Social and Behavioural Research (GJSBR)**: The GJSBR is designed to serve as a platform for global perspectives on social and behavioural research. It encourages submissions that explore the interconnected nature of human behaviour and societal changes worldwide.
- **Nwaebere Journal of Social and Behavioural Research (NJSBR)**: Named to honour IMSU heritage, this journal focuses on innovative research inspired by African contexts while remaining relevant to global audiences. It bridges regional insights with broader academic discussions.

Our goal is to create a robust and inclusive platform for researchers, scholars, and practitioners to share knowledge, collaborate, and contribute to solving pressing social and behavioural issues. Through these journals, we strive to promote excellence in research, uphold ethical standards, and ensure the dissemination of valuable insights to a global audience.

## INFORMATION FOR AUTHORS

### Manuscript Preparation Guidelines:
- **Formatting:** Manuscripts must be prepared using Microsoft Word, formatted on A4-sized paper, with 1-inch margins on all sides. The text should be double-spaced and written in 12-point Times New Roman font.
- **Length:** Articles should not exceed 25 pages (8,000 words), including tables, figures, and references.
- **Content Structure:** The manuscript should include:
  - A cover page with the title of the paper and author details (name, affiliation, email, and phone number). The authors' names must be clearly stated in the correct order (i.e. first name, middle name, and surname) with institutional addresses, email addresses and phone contact provided as well.
  - An abstract of no more than 250 words summarising the purpose, methods, findings, and significance of the study.
  - The main body of the manuscript, including an introduction, methodology, results, discussion, and conclusion.
  - Tables, figures, and diagrams numbered sequentially and referred to in the text.
  - References must be formatted according to APA 7th Edition guidelines.

### Submission Process:
- Manuscripts can be submitted via email to `fossjournals@gmail.com` or `ajsbs2016@gmail.com`, which will guide authors through the process.
- Submissions must include all required documents and adhere to the journal's formatting rules to proceed to the review stage.

### Peer Review Policy:
- All submissions undergo a rigorous double-blind peer review process to ensure unbiased evaluation.
- Reviewers are selected based on their expertise in the subject area of the manuscript.
- Feedback is provided to authors, and manuscripts may be accepted, revised, or rejected based on the reviewers’ recommendations.

### Ethical Considerations:
- Authors must ensure their work is original and free from plagiarism.
- Proper acknowledgement must be given to all sources, and ethical approval must be secured for studies involving human or animal participants.
- Conflicts of interest must be disclosed, and authors are required to adhere to the highest ethical standards in research and publication.

### Publication Fees:
- A non-refundable review fee of **₦10,000** (or equivalent in USD for international authors) is required upon submission.
- Upon acceptance, a publication fee of **₦25,000** (or equivalent in USD for international authors) must be paid before the article is published.

## REVIEW AND SUBMISSION PROCESS

- **Step 1: Manuscript Submission**  
  Authors submit their manuscripts via the designated email. A cover letter must accompany the manuscript, stating its originality and relevance to the journal's scope.
- **Step 2: Initial Assessment**  
  The editorial team reviews submissions to ensure they meet the journal's requirements in terms of formatting, scope, and quality. Submissions that fail this initial screening will be returned for correction.
- **Step 3: Peer Review**  
  Manuscripts are anonymised and sent to at least two reviewers for a thorough evaluation. Reviewers assess submissions based on originality, methodology, clarity, and relevance to the journal’s focus.
- **Step 4: Reviewer Recommendations**  
  Reviewers recommend one of the following outcomes:
  - Accept: The manuscript is ready for publication.
  - Minor Revisions: The manuscript requires small adjustments to meet publication standards.
  - Major Revisions: Substantial changes are needed, and the revised manuscript must be reviewed again.
  - Reject: The manuscript does not meet the standards for publication.
- **Step 5: Revision and Resubmission**  
  Authors receive feedback and are given a timeline to revise and resubmit their manuscript. Revisions are reviewed to ensure all issues have been addressed.
- **Step 6: Final Decision**  
  The editorial team makes the final decision based on reviewer evaluations. Accepted manuscripts proceed to the production stage.
- **Step 7: Post-Publication**  
  Published articles are indexed online for easy access. Authors can track citations and share their work with the academic community.
